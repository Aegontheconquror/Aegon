FARM GUIDES

Make informed decisions at every stage.

Use these starter guides to structure your research. Adapt every recommendation to your location, soil, climate, budget and current agricultural advice.

01. Planning a maize farm

Choose suitable seed for your area, test soil where possible, prepare the land, plan input costs and keep records of planting and harvesting dates.

02. Preparing a poultry enterprise

Research your target market before buying birds. Budget for housing, feed, clean water, vaccination, biosecurity, bedding and emergency expenses.

03. Managing farm finances

Separate household and farm money, record every expense, estimate realistic selling prices and retain working capital for the next production cycle.

04. Finding market information

Compare local buyers, transport costs, produce quality requirements and current market prices before deciding when and where to sell.
